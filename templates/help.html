Frequently Asked Questions

Why is accuracy high?

Accuracy is high because the dataset is formula-based and the selected model learned the relationship effectively.

What is feature importance?

Feature importance tells which input parameter affects prediction the most. Tree-based models provide this directly.

How can I reduce power?

Reduce voltage, lower frequency, and optimize capacitance values. These strongly affect total power.

What does sensitivity analysis show?

It shows how predicted power changes when voltage is increased by a small amount.

Why does voltage matter so much?

Dynamic power depends on voltage square, so even a small increase in voltage can cause noticeable power increase.

Which model is used in the project?

The system compares models and uses the best-performing model for prediction.
